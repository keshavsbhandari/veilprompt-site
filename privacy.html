Privacy Policy

VeilPrompt · Last updated 30 October 2026

YOUR PII STAYS WITH YOU. VeilPrompt runs in your browser. It does not send your messages, personal data, or stand-in tables to any server, because VeilPrompt has no server.

What VeilPrompt does

VeilPrompt is a browser extension. Before a message you type into ChatGPT is sent, it finds personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, bank account numbers, national ID numbers, API keys, and addresses. It replaces each value with a realistic stand-in. When the reply comes back, it puts your real values back on your screen.

Where your data is processed

All detection and replacement happen on your device. Pattern checks run in the extension. Names, addresses, and cities are found by a small language model that also runs on your device.

What the extension stores, and where

  • Settings (which kinds of data to hide, your allow list, whether to anonymize automatically, and whether the model is on) are stored in your browser's local extension storage.
  • The real-to-stand-in table for each conversation is stored in your browser's session storage. It is removed when the browser session ends. It is never uploaded, and VeilPrompt does not keep a copy outside your browser.
  • A record of each input and its anonymized output is kept with that conversation's table, in the same session storage, so repeated inputs do not need to be processed again.

Network requests

VeilPrompt makes one kind of network request. When you press Download model on the setup page, it downloads a one-time detection model of about 58 MB from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). After that the model is cached in your browser and no further download is needed. VeilPrompt has no other connection to any server, and it does not send your text anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage data.
  • We do not have accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data, because we do not receive any.
  • We do not read your chats outside the chat page where VeilPrompt is active.

Replies and what you can recover

While VeilPrompt is installed and the browser session lasts, you see replies with your real values put back. If you remove the extension, clear the browser storage that holds the table, or the session ends, you will see only the masked output. The original values cannot be recovered from the chat, because we do not store the table anywhere else.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your settings and the per-conversation tables described above.
  • offscreen: runs the local detection model in a hidden browser page.
  • Site access (chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com): reads and changes the message box and reply text on those pages, so personal data can be replaced before sending and restored in replies.

Children

VeilPrompt is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
