VeilPrompt

Swap the real names out before the chat sees them.

VeilPrompt is a browser extension that replaces personal data in your messages with realistic stand-ins before they leave your browser. When the reply comes back, it puts your real values back on your screen.

YOUR PII STAYS WITH YOU. Detection runs on your device. VeilPrompt has no server and does not upload your messages.

How it works

  1. Detect. Emails, phone numbers, card numbers, bank accounts, national IDs, API keys, and IP addresses are found by pattern checks. Names, addresses, and cities are found by a small local model.
  2. Stand in. Each value is replaced with one of the same kind, such as a card number that passes the Luhn check.
  3. Restore. The reply is shown with your real values again, while VeilPrompt is installed.

What to expect

  • Detection is not perfect. Check the highlighted items before you send. You decide what gets hidden: turn any item off, or add values to the allow list.
  • The first time you enable names and addresses, VeilPrompt downloads a model of about 58 MB from Hugging Face. After that it is cached in your browser.
  • Works on ChatGPT (chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com) in this first release.
